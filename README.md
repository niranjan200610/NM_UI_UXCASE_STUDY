# NM_UI_UXCASE_STUDY
A modern Figma travel app that helps users discover destinations, plan trips, and explore cultures easily. Features clean UI, detailed wireframes, and interactive prototypes for Android/iOS focused on smart itineraries and seamless travel.
💪 FitLife – Your Fitness, Your Way.
A Figma fitness app promoting wellness with workouts, meal tracking, and progress insights. Designed with clean UI, smart analytics, and motivational flow for users of all fitness levels.

🎵 MusicStream – Smart Streaming. Infinite Sound.
A sleek Figma music app with personalized playlists, mood-based recommendations, and immersive UI. Includes high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes for a smooth streaming experience.
# 🎨 Figma Design Projects Showcase

This repository contains three professional UI/UX design concepts created in *Figma* — focused on travel, fitness, and music experiences. Each project demonstrates wireframes, high-fidelity screens, and interactive prototypes for Android and iOS.

### 🧭 TravelMate – Smart Travel. Infinite Adventures.

> “Explore smarter, travel farther.”

A sleek *travel and exploration app* built in *Figma*, designed to help users discover destinations, plan trips, and explore cultures effortlessly.  
Features minimalist UI, smooth interactions, and vibrant travel imagery — focused on *personalized itineraries* and *smart exploration*.

*✨ Highlights*
- Clean, modern UI/UX for Android & iOS  
- Interactive wireframes & prototypes  
- Smart itinerary planner and trip recommendations  

*🛠 Tools Used:* Figma Prototyping UI/UX Design

### 💪 FitLife – Your Fitness, Your Way.

> “Train smart. Live strong.”

A *fitness and lifestyle mobile app* designed to inspire wellness and balance. Created in *Figma*, it features intuitive workout tracking, nutrition guidance, and progress analytics with motivational UI design.

*✨ Highlights*
- Workout tracking & meal planner  
- Progress visualization dashboards  
- Simple, motivating, user-friendly flow  

*🛠 Tools Used:* Figma UI Design Health & Wellness

### 🎵 MusicStream – Smart Streaming. Infinite Sound.

> “Stream your vibe.”

A stylish *music streaming app concept* emphasizing discovery, personalization, and immersive sound. Created with Figma to feature *mood-based playlists, **curated music feeds, and a stunning **Now Playing* interface.

- Mood-based playlists & smart recommendations  
- Immersive player with dynamic visuals
